What the plan covers

Health Canada requires more than a business plan. We write everything they need to see.

01

Security Narrative

Health Canada's application requires a detailed security plan covering physical security measures, personnel security, and cannabis tracking. We document your site security to the specific standards the Cannabis Tracking and Licensing System requires.

02

Site and Facility Plan

Detailed documentation of your cultivation, processing, or storage facility including floor plans, access control, and environmental controls. Structured to match Health Canada's facility assessment criteria for your licence class.

03

Standard Operating Procedures

Cannabis licence applications require written SOPs for cultivation, harvesting, processing, destruction, and record-keeping. We draft the full SOP package to the depth Health Canada's compliance reviewers expect to see.

04

Financial Projections

Three-year financial model including production capacity, revenue projections by product category, operating cost structure, and capital expenditure schedule. Formatted for both Health Canada and any lender financing the operation.

05

Market and Distribution Plan

Canadian cannabis market analysis, provincial distribution channel documentation, and your go-to-market strategy by product form. Written to demonstrate that your business model is viable under the Cannabis Act's distribution framework.

06

Quality Assurance Framework

Documentation of your quality assurance program, Good Production Practices compliance approach, and testing protocols. Structured to satisfy both Health Canada's requirements and any third-party audit framework your distribution partner requires.

Who it's for

For cannabis entrepreneurs navigating a compliance-heavy licensing process.

Micro-cultivators

Small-scale cultivators applying for a Health Canada micro-cultivation licence. The micro-licence application has the same documentation requirements as a standard licence, in a smaller facility context. We know what Health Canada looks for at this scale.

Standard cultivators and processors

Larger operations seeking standard cultivation or processing licences. The application requires detailed facility documentation, SOP packages, and a full security plan. We have the template structures and regulatory knowledge to produce these efficiently.

Cannabis retailers

Cannabis retail store applicants in provinces where retail licences require a business plan submission. Includes Ontario, BC, and Alberta retail plan formats. We also write plans for retail financings where a lender requires a formal document alongside the retail licence.
